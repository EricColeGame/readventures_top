## Core Combat Roles for Readventures Units

To build an adaptable squad, you cannot rely solely on raw offensive numbers. Successful defense calls for balanced team construction where every member fulfills a designated role on the map. In the current ecosystem, readventures units are broadly grouped into five major categories:

1. **Ground Frontliners:** Placed along floor pathways to intercept dense swarms and fast runners.
2. **Hill Snipers:** Placed on elevated terrain, generally targeting airborne threats and high-priority targets with long range.
3. **Economy Generators:** Provide steady passive income every wave to finance high-cost unit upgrades.
4. **Crowd Controllers (CC):** Apply slows, freezes, knockbacks, or stuns to compress enemy waves.
5. **Support Buffers:** Increase teammate attack speed, range, or raw damage output within their operational aura.

| Role Archetype | Optimal Placement | Damage Pattern | Priority Stats | Role Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Carry (DPS)** | Bends / Chokepoints | Full Circle / Huge Cone AoE | Attack, Attack Speed | Wipe standard mob swarms |
| **Boss Shredder** | Central High Ground | Single Target / Line Piercing | Critical Chance, Boss Dmg | Melt high-HP single targets |
| **Cash Generator** | Safe Backline / Corners | None | Cost Reduction, Income/Wave | Accelerate squad upgrades |
| **Debuff / Stall** | Early Track Intersection | Lingering Field AoE | Range, Cooldown Reduction | Delay fast-moving runners |
| **Aura Buffer** | Clustered Near Carries | Non-Damaging Field | Range, Ability Duration | Boost adjacent damage dealers |

Understanding where your units can be placed is vital. Hill spots are limited on many challenge maps, meaning an over-reliance on Hill-only readventures units will leave your deployment slots congested and your ground lanes vulnerable to leaks.

## Evolution, Traits, and Unit Progression

Acquiring a powerful character is only the first step. To unlock the full potential of your readventures units, you must progress them through evolutionary trees and roll for optimal passive traits. Evolution typically alters character cosmetics while massively boosting their baseline scaling, adding additional attack phases, and expanding their attack radius.

### The Evolution Process

Evolving units requires collecting specific planetary fragments, stage drops, and sacrifice materials from daily challenge maps. Keeping a stockpile of standard ascension stones guarantees you can immediately evolve top-tier summons whenever a new banner drops.

| Evolution Tier | Required Catalyst Types | Average Stat Increase | Added Mechanical Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Form** | Initial Summon | Baseline (100%) | Standard auto-attack |
| **Ascended (Tier 1)** | World Essences + Common Stones | +40% Attack, +15% Range | Unlocks Secondary Attack Animation |
| **Awakened (Tier 2)** | Raid Drops + Mythic Remnants | +85% Attack, +30% Range | Adds Elemental Status Effect (Burn/Freeze) |
| **Mastered (Final)** | Exclusive Event Shards + Gold | +150% Attack, Lower Cooldown | Full Screen Ultimate / Active Ability |

### Rolling and Managing Traits

Traits act as permanent passive multipliers rolled through trait tokens. A unit with an S-tier trait can easily outperform a higher-tier unit stuck with sub-optimal rolls. Player experience shows that securing traits such as **Nimble** (attack speed bonus), **Precision** (critical rate boost), or rare unique modifiers like **Divine/Monarch** drastically alters unit viability.

* **Priority 1:** Reroll traits only on your permanent Mythic carries or specialized farm units.
* **Priority 2:** Avoid burning rare tokens on temporary early-game placeholders.
* **Priority 3:** Prioritize Range and Attack Speed over minor flat damage boosts on support and debuff units.

## Optimal Squad Compositions and Tactical Deployment

Constructing a balanced team of six readventures units requires a structured plan for each phase of a match. A common pitfall is packing a team full of expensive Mythic units, which leads to immediate defeat on wave 2 or 3 because you lack the starting funds to deploy them.

### Wave-by-Wave Deployment Strategy

To get the most out of your readventures units during a match, follow this standard deployment sequence:

* **Waves 1–3 (Setup Phase):** Place your lowest-cost ground attacker near an early turn to handle opening mobs. Immediately place your first economic unit as soon as funds permit.
* **Waves 4–8 (Economic Ramp):** Upgrade your farm unit rather than over-investing in attack units. Only upgrade your defender if enemies begin crossing past the halfway mark of your track.
* **Waves 9–15 (Mid-Game Transition):** Drop your primary Hill or Ground AoE carry at the most efficient chokepoint. Maximize their first two upgrade tiers to unlock wider attack arcs.
* **Waves 16+ (Endgame Lockdown):** Position support buffers to cover your primary carries, layer your crowd control units at the front of the track, and direct target priority settings (First, Strongest, or Last) according to current wave threats.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Readventures Units

### What are the best readventures units for beginners?
For players just starting out, focus on high-efficiency Epic and Legendary units with low placement costs and circular AoE attacks. Units with reliable bleed or burn mechanics provide consistent damage without needing massive cash investments during early story chapters. Combine one cheap early carry with a basic income generator to smooth out your learning curve.

### How do I get more trait tokens to reroll my readventures units?
Trait tokens are primarily earned by completing daily quests, finishing infinite mode milestones, and participating in seasonal events. You can also acquire them through dungeon shops using reward currency. Community reports recommend saving tokens until you pull a top-tier Mythic unit rather than dispersing them across temporary squad members.

### Should I prioritize evolving my support units or my main carries first?
Always prioritize evolving your main damage carries first. Carries gain substantial DPS increases, expanded range, and essential status afflictions from evolution that are required to clear higher raid tiers. Support units generally perform their primary duties—such as slowing enemies or providing flat stat auras—efficiently even in their base forms.

### Why are my readventures units missing fast-moving enemies?
If enemies are running past your defenders, your units likely suffer from slow attack animations (long attack cooldowns) or lack sufficient crowd control support. Adding a dedicated slow or stun character near the start of the path groups enemies together, allowing your heavy-hitting readventures units to land their full skill rotations accurately.
