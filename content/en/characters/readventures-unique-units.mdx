## Team Synergy: Building Around Your Main Carry

No unit operates in a vacuum. The most potent team compositions support the strengths of your primary **readventures unique units** while masking their weaknesses, such as slow attack animations or high initial placement costs.

### Essential Roster Roles
* **The Hyper-Carry:** One primary unique AoE unit (e.g., Crow Illusionist) that handles 70% or more of total wave damage.
* **The Single-Target Executioner:** A rapid-attack piercing unit dedicated to cutting down elite bosses and high-health targets before they exit range.
* **The Income Generator:** A dedicated cash-generation unit that funds rapid carry upgrades before wave 20.
* **The Buffer:** A support character providing aura-based attack speed increases or damage boosts to adjacent allies.
* **The Crowd Controller:** A freeze or stun specialist placed at high-traffic choke points to cluster enemies into your carry's kill zone.

Positioning your support buffers within range of both your AoE carry and your single-target boss melter multiplies your total defensive output without requiring extra upgrade costs. Always verify your range rings during placement so that no unit footprint goes underutilized.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are readventures unique units and why are they important?
**Readventures unique units** are top-tier summons and characters equipped with exceptional stat growth, strict placement restrictions, and battle-defining passives. They form the backbone of late-game infinite runs, high-tier raids, and speedrun farming setups.

### Can you place more than one Unique unit at a time?
Standard game mechanics generally restrict unique units to a placement cap of one per player during a match. However, if your unit rolls the specific "Unique" passive trait during rerolling, community reports confirm that you gain an additional placement slot, allowing you to deploy two copies of that carry simultaneously.

### Should I prioritize evolving my unique unit or rerolling traits first?
Always evolve your unique unit first. Evolution provides a guaranteed, massive boost to base damage, range, and passive effects, whereas trait rerolling relies on randomized probabilities. Once your unit is fully evolved and at maximum level, spend your spare resources rerolling for top-tier traits like Unique, Celestial, or Reaper.

### What is the most effective element for unique units in raid content?
Elemental effectiveness varies based on the specific raid boss you face, but Dark, Fire, and Lightning archetypes generally offer the highest versatility. Dark and Fire apply high-scaling percentage burns that ignore heavy armor ratings, while Lightning delivers dependable crowd-control interruptions against fast-moving bosses.
